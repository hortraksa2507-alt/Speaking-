Quick reminders for test day

  • Part 1: Answer + reason + example. Don't stop after one sentence.
  • Part 2: Your goal is the process, not winning. Suggest → respond → compromise → confirm the decision out loud.
  • Part 3: Give an opinion, support it, then pass the turn with a question. Comparing Cambodia / Japan / Australia is your S6 superpower — use it once or twice per topic, naturally.

Tip: record yourself, listen back, and repeat the same task until it feels easy. Good luck in your exam! 🍀
